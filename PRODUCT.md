# Safe Somewhere

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

People who stumble on the imprint — listeners, artists, curious passersby. Not hiring managers, not a “label catalog shopper” yet.

## Purpose

A one-page presence for Safe Somewhere: a collective / imprint world where projects and ideas can live. Short synopsis of what it is. Quiet arrival, not a hype machine.

Success: the visitor feels they have arrived somewhere. Soft social contact is available if they want it.

## Positioning

Not everything is safe — but somewhere still is. Music (and the projects around it) as refuge, not therapy or escape. Wider and more permanent than Who’s John; the artist can live inside this world without owning the whole identity.

Collective first, “record label” only as flair — not a conventional release-hype site.

## Brand personality

Ominous · quiet · restrained

Minimal but fresh. Invite, don’t scream. Soft contact only.

## Anti-references

- Who’s John EPK clone (Syne/Bebas, neon fluid text, panel fader)
- Wellness / therapy / “safe space” pastels
- Generic techno-label chrome
- Motivational / healing promises
- Hands holding hearts, spa imagery, smiling stock photos

## Conversion & proof

- **Primary CTA:** Arrive — leave with the feeling that this place exists
- **Secondary CTA:** Soft socials — Instagram `safe.somewhere` · TikTok `@safesomewhere` · YouTube `safesomewhere`
- **First viewport:** Brand wordmark only (no body line under it)
- **Layout cue:** kntxt.be-style full-bleed, text around the edges (`matte` · `safe` · `somewhere`)
- **Memorable after 10s:** This place exists; you can stay a while
- **Belief ladder:** (1) something is here → (2) it’s a refuge, not a pitch → (3) I can follow if I want
- **Proof:** None required for v1 — presence is the proof

## Accessibility & inclusion

Standard web basics (contrast, keyboard focus, reduced motion). Not a focus for v1.
